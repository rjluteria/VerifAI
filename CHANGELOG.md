## Known limitations

The target remains 30 seconds for healthy warm fresh verification. Recorded fresh runs can take minutes. Reviewer property confusion, source access limitations, provider failures and cold starts are disclosed in the release notes and acceptance reports. Historical gold, failed runs and rollback revisions were not rewritten.
